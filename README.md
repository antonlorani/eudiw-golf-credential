EUDI sets a new standard for interoperable verifable credentials within the EU.  It enables users safely share their digital identity to third-parties that must request this information for either legal or organisatory reasons.

Most use-cases for EUDI are eIDAS specific, such as legimitation when creating a bank account or wanting to borrow money from a financial institute or prooving ones legal age to age restricted platforms. 

This project's use-case is all about Golf.

Did you know that the majority of golf clubs want a proof of your hcp (handicap) score before you can play at it? That's right! And your HCP is bound to your legal identity as well! And EUDI has a way to represent this kind of information!

This project implements the OID4VCI and OID4VP protocols for issuing and verifying SD JWT VC credentials into any EUDI compatible Wallet.


One benefit of EUDI is reducing the efforts of integrating every non-standardized national e-ID scheme. Interestingly, the game of Golf has the same problem.

If you want to play at a golf course you first have to proof to that golf course that you:
1. Carry a HCP - A proof that you know the golf etiquetes about behavior/course care/pace of play
2. Have a HCP below a certain threshold (mostly 36.0)

This information is useful to ensure fair tournaments and a smooth game of golf for everyone (There's nothing worse than stuck in front of a slowly playing flight).

Your personal HCP information is tracked by your national golf association. This tracking has been largerly digitalized already across the EU, BUT within national borders:

This creates the same difficulty as for the national e-ID schemes, if you want to play at a golf course outside of national borders, you have to carry over all your golf membership information to that national golf association and of course the economical/practical benefit for a golf course or booking platform to support all digital golf membership schemes doesn't work. 

EUDI can fix that.

Since its EU-wide standardized, a player can issue their national golf membership credential into their national EUDI Wallet, and a golf course or booking platform can verify that credential using any EUDI compatible OID4VP integration.
